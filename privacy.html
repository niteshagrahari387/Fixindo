Privacy policy

Your information and privacy.

Effective date: July 2026

FIXINDO values your privacy and is committed to protecting personal information.

Information we collect

Customers may provide their name, mobile number, email address, location with permission, and optional profile photo.

Professionals may provide their name, business details, contact information, business address, categories, working area, and business photos.

Aadhaar information is used only for verification; the application stores only the last four digits.

How we use information

We use it to create accounts, connect customers and professionals, improve search accuracy, send booking notifications, verify businesses, improve security, and prevent fraud.

Location and sharing

Location permission is requested only to show nearby professionals and improve recommendations.

FIXINDO does not sell personal information. Limited details may be shared between a customer and selected professional, where required by law, or to improve platform functionality.

Your rights

You may update your profile, delete your account, request removal of personal data, or contact us with privacy concerns.

This policy should be reviewed by a legal professional before large-scale data processing, payments, or KYC operations.
